Who This Is For

A Reference, Not Just a Brochure

Industrial Thermal Design Resources is curated by Powerblanket's Engineering and Applications Team — the same engineers who design and build the heating systems discussed here. We built it for two audiences at once: customers working through a real heating problem, and anyone simply curious about how industrial thermal design actually works.

Every guide is written the way we'd explain it to an engineer sitting across the table — real physics, real trade-offs, real photos from real installations. Powerblanket builds the products discussed here, and we say so plainly because we'd rather earn your trust than disguise it.

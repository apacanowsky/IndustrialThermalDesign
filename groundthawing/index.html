Ground Thawing

Thawing frozen ground ahead of excavation, construction, and pipeline work in cold climates.

Why It Matters

Frozen Ground Stops the Job Before It Starts

Excavation, utility work, and pipeline projects can't proceed until frozen ground thaws and frost is cleared ahead of a pour. Digging into frozen soil burns labor hours and wears out equipment that wasn't built for it, and concrete poured in cold weather without the right cure conditions loses strength — a compromise that shows up years later, not on the day of the pour.
